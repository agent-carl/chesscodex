The Belgrade Gambit is 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.d4 exd4 5.Nd5: in the Scotch Variation of the [Four Knights Game](/openings/four-knights-game) White does not take back on d4 but plays the knight to d5 instead, leaving the pawn for the moment.

### The idea

The knight on d5 attacks the knight on f6, and White leaves the pawn on d4 in Black's hands for the moment. Black's most common replies are 5...Be7, 5...Nb4, 5...Nxd5 and 5...Nxe4.

### Main lines

- 5...Be7 6.Bf4 d6 7.Nxd4 O-O 8.Nb5 Nxd5 9.exd5 Ne5 is the main line.
- 5...Nb4. White usually answers 6.Bc4, 6.Nxf6 or 6.Nxd4. After 6.Nxd4 Nbxd5 7.exd5 Nxd5 Black is a pawn up, and both sides have only one knight developed. 6...Nxe4 avoids the exchange of knights.
- 5...Nxd5 6.exd5 Bb4+ 7.Bd2 Qe7+ 8.Qe2 Bxd2+ 9.Kxd2 Qxe2+ 10.Bxe2, followed by 10...Ne7 or 10...Nb4: an early queen exchange.
- [5...Nxe4](/openings/four-knights-game-scotch-variation-belgrade-gambit-modern-defense) 6.Qe2 f5 is a more modern continuation. White also has 6.Bc4 and 6.Bd3.

The move 4.Nd5, the Naroditsky Variation, has a similar idea.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 54.4% of the 220,000 games and Black 40.0%. Under 1400 Black is slightly ahead, 48.4% against 47.9%, but from 1400 up White wins clearly: 55.3% against 39.2% between 1800 and 2199, and 50.9% against 41.2% from 2200 up. In 484 master games over the board Black won 32.6%, White 22.5%, and 44.8% were drawn.
