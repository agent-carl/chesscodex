The Exchange Variation of the [Queen's Gambit Declined](/openings/queens-gambit-declined) (1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.Nc3 d5 4.cxd5, or 1.d4 d5 2.c4 e6 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.cxd5) fixes the central pawn structure at once. After 4...exd5 White has a pawn majority in the centre and Black one on the queenside.

### The idea

The structure gives White two plans: advance the centre pawns with Nge2, f3 and e4, or launch a minority attack with Rb1, b4 and b5, aiming to leave Black with a weak pawn on c6. For Black, the exchange has freed the light-squared bishop and opened the e-file, with e4 as a springboard for central and kingside play. Chances are balanced, but because the long-term features of the structure favour White, Black usually has to use the extra piece activity for an attack on White's king. Model games for White are Evans–Opsahl, Dubrovnik 1950 (minority attack) and Carlsen–Jakovenko, Nanjing 2009 (central advance).

### Main lines

- 4...exd5 5.Bg5, the [Positional Variation](/openings/queens-gambit-declined-exchange-variation-positional-variation), threatening 6.Bxf6 and 7.Nxd5. The usual replies 5...c6 and 5...Be7 normally transpose; the main line continues 6.e3 Be7 7.Bd3 Nbd7 8.Qc2 0-0, followed by Nf3 or Nge2. 6.Qc2 is the [Reshevsky Variation](/openings/queens-gambit-declined-exchange-variation-reshevsky-variation).
- Lines with ...h6 and Bh4, such as the [Carlsen Variation](/openings/queens-gambit-declined-exchange-variation-carlsen-variation) with ...Bg4.
- 4...Nxd5, which usually transposes to the [Semi-Tarrasch Defense](/openings/queens-gambit-declined-semi-tarrasch-defense) after 5.e4 Nxc3 6.bxc3 c5 7.Nf3.

### Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths**

- Clear, well-understood plans for White: the minority attack or a central advance.
- The long-term features of the structure favour White.

**Weaknesses**

- Black's light-squared bishop is freed and the e-file opens.
- Black gets active pieces and chances for a kingside attack.

### Who should play it

The Exchange Variation suits White players who like clear strategic plans such as the minority attack.

### The numbers

On Lichess White scores about 51–52% at every level: 50.7% against 45.5% under 1400, 51.9% against 43.7% at 1400–1799, 52.1% against 42.2% at 1800–2199 and 51.1% against 40.9% from 2200 up. In about 18,400 master games White won 35.7%, Black only 16.6%, and 47.7% were drawn.
