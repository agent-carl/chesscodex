The Blackmar-Diemer Gambit Accepted is 1.d4 d5 2.e4 dxe4 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.f3 exf3, the main line of the [Blackmar-Diemer Gambit](/openings/blackmar-diemer-gambit). Black takes the second pawn offered, and White gets a tempo and a half-open f-file in return for a pawn. The German master Emil Josef Diemer (1908–1990) popularised 3...Nf6 4.f3.

### After 5.Nxf3

White usually recaptures with the knight, and Black then has five main options:

- 5...Bf5, the Gunderam Defence, analysed extensively by Gerhart Gunderam. White's main reply is 6.Ne5, aiming to chase the bishop with the kingside pawns.
- 5...Bg4, the Teichmann Defence, pins the knight, often to exchange it and undermine White's centre. White's best answer is 6.h3.
- 5...e6, the Euwe Defence, analysed by Max Euwe, aims for a French-type position with an extra pawn. Play usually continues 6.Bg5 Be7, and White's most popular move is 7.Bd3.
- 5...g6, the Bogoljubov Defence, fianchettoes the bishop to increase pressure on d4 after a later ...c5. Diemer himself played it against Bogoljubov.
- 5...c6, the Ziegler Defence, is Black's most critical reply, and most modern authors recommend it as the antidote to the gambit.

### 5.Qxf3

White can also recapture with the queen and offer a second pawn, the [Ryder Gambit](/openings/blackmar-diemer-gambit-accepted-ryder-gambit). Most of Diemer's book on the opening, published in the late 1950s, was devoted to it.

### Assessment

The soundness of the gambit is much debated. Many masters dismiss it while many amateurs embrace it, and theory suggests Black has many ways to equalise, so it is almost never seen in top-level play but has a certain popularity among club players. IM Willy Hendriks noted that this main line, after 5.Nxf3, has the same engine evaluation as the King's Gambit after 1.e4 e5 2.f4 exf4 3.Nf3.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 52.8% of the 2.4 million games and Black 43.5%, and White stays ahead in every rating band. Masters have done far better with Black: in 240 games over the board Black won 42.1%, White 19.6%, and 38.3% were drawn.
