The Quiet Variation of the [Slav Defense](/openings/slav-defense), also called the Slow Slav, is 1.d4 d5 2.c4 c6 3.Nf3 Nf6 4.e3. With 4.e3 White avoids the complexities of the main line, 4.Nc3.

### Main line: 4...Bf5

The most common continuation is 4...Bf5 5.Nc3 e6 6.Nh4. White wins the bishop pair, but Black gets a solid position and often counterplay with ...e5. This line was tested several times in the 2006 World Chess Championship.

### Other lines

After 4...Bf5, 5.cxd5 cxd5 6.Qb3 Qc7 is fine for Black. White tries to exploit the absence of Black's light-squared bishop from the queenside, but this is not enough for an advantage if Black plays accurately.

Another way to play for Black is 4...Bg4.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 48.8% of the 2.7 million games and Black 44.6%. From 2200 up White's lead grows to 50.1% against 40.9%, with 9.0% drawn. Masters draw more than half their games: in 29,404 games over the board 54.8% were drawn, White won 29.1% and Black 16.1%.
