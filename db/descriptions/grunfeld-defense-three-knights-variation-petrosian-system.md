The Petrosian System of the [Grünfeld Defense](/openings/grunfeld-defense), also called the Taimanov Variation, is 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 g6 3.Nc3 d5 4.Nf3 Bg7 5.Bg5. Yasser Seirawan has favoured this line.

### 5...Ne4

Black's reply is nearly always 5...Ne4. White then plays 6.Bh4 or 6.cxd5.

- After 6.Bh4 Black can play 6...Nxc3 7.bxc3 dxc4.
- After 6.cxd5 Black usually plays 6...Nxg5 7.Nxg5 e6; 7...c6 is sometimes tried instead.
- Grabbing the pawn with 6.Nxd5 loses a piece after 6...Nxg5 7.Nxg5 e6.
- After 6.cxd5 Nxg5 7.Nxg5 e6 White has 8.Qd2 exd5 9.Qe3+, with attacking chances; the interpolation 8...h6 9.Nf3 exd5 is a significant alternative for Black.

### The usual structure

The more usual 8.Nf3 exd5 leads to play like the [Exchange Variation](/openings/queens-gambit-declined-exchange-variation) of the Queen's Gambit Declined. White carries out a minority attack on the queenside with b2–b4–b5 and bxc6, while Black aims for the traditional kingside play with f7–f5–f4 and, in this case, g6–g5.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 48.1% of the 750,786 games and Black 45.9%. From 2200 up White's lead grows to 48.7% against 42.3%. Among masters the results are nearly even: in 3,881 games over the board White won 28.5%, Black 27.4%, and 44.1% were drawn.
