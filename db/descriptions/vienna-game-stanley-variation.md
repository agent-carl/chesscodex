The Stanley Variation is 1.e4 e5 2.Nc3 Nf6 3.Bc4, a line of the [Vienna Game](/openings/vienna-game) in which White develops the bishop instead of playing f2-f4. The same position can be reached from the [Bishop's Opening](/openings/bishops-opening), 2.Bc4 Nf6 3.Nc3.

### Black's main choices

- 3...Nxe4 takes the pawn at once (see below).
- 3...Nc6 usually continues 4.d3, the [Berlin-Vienna Hybrid](/openings/bishops-opening-vienna-hybrid), followed by 4...Na5, 4...Bc5 or 4...Bb4.
- 3...Bc5 transposes to the King's Gambit Declined after 4.d3 d6 5.f4 Nc6 6.Nf3.
- [3...Bb4](/openings/vienna-game-stanley-variation-reversed-spanish), after which 4.f4 Nxe4 5.Qh5 O-O leads to wild but probably equal play. White can also play 4.d3, 4.Nge2 or 4.Nf3.

### After 3...Nxe4

White usually plays 4.Qh5, threatening Qxf7#, and Black almost always replies 4...Nd6. After 5.Bb3 Black chooses between the quieter 5...Be7 6.Nf3 Nc6 7.Nxe5, the [Alekhine Variation](/openings/vienna-game-stanley-variation-alekhine-variation), and the complications of 5...Nc6 6.Nb5 g6 7.Qf3 f5 8.Qd5 Qe7 9.Nxc7+ Kd8 10.Nxa8 b6, which Tim Harding dubbed the [Frankenstein-Dracula Variation](/openings/vienna-game-frankenstein-dracula-variation). White can also trade queens with 5.Qxe5+ Qe7 6.Qxe7+ Bxe7.

Of the other fourth moves, 4.Nxe4 allows the fork 4...d5 and 4.Bxf7+ is considered weak. 4.Nf3 is another option, entering the [Boden-Kieseritzky Gambit](/openings/bishops-opening-boden-kieseritzky-gambit).

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 50.4% of the 6.2 million games and Black 45.1%. The split is similar at every rating: 50.5% against 45.6% under 1400 and 48.8% against 44.0% from 2200 up. Masters over the board split their decisive games evenly: in 2,047 games White won 28.6%, Black 28.0%, and 43.4% were drawn.
