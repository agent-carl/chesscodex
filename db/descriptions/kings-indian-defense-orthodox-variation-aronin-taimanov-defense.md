The Aronin-Taimanov Defense is 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 g6 3.Nc3 Bg7 4.e4 d6 5.Nf3 O-O 6.Be2 e5 7.O-O Nc6, the main line of the [Classical (Orthodox) Variation](/openings/kings-indian-defense-orthodox-variation) of the [King's Indian Defense](/openings/kings-indian-defense). With 8.d5 Ne7 it becomes the line known as the Mar del Plata Variation.

### The idea

After 8.d5 the centre is closed and both sides attack on opposite wings. White prepares the queenside break c4-c5. Black brings the f6-knight to d7, where it also helps slow down c4-c5, and starts a kingside pawn storm with ...f5-f4 and ...g5.

### Main lines

After 8.d5 Ne7 White has a wide choice of ninth moves:

- [9.b4](/openings/kings-indian-defense-orthodox-variation-bayonet-attack), the Bayonet Attack, introduced by Viktor Korchnoi in the 1970s. For a while it put top players off the whole line, and Teimour Radjabov has recently revived it.
- [9.Ne1](/openings/kings-indian-defense-orthodox-variation-classical-system).
- [9.Nd2](/openings/kings-indian-defense-orthodox-variation-modern-system).

Black's other seventh moves are 7...Nbd7, the old main line, which is less common nowadays; 7...exd4 8.Nxd4, popularised by Igor Glek in the mid-1990s, though White still keeps an advantage in most lines; and 7...Na6, which has gained some popularity recently. White can avoid the whole system with [7.d5](/openings/kings-indian-defense-petrosian-variation), the Petrosian Variation, or 7.Be3, the Gligorić System.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 51.4% of the 1 million games and Black 43.2%. White's lead grows with rating: 49.7% against 46.5% under 1400 and 52.1% against 41.5% from 2200 up. In 20,115 master games over the board White won 36.1%, Black 21.4%, and 42.5% were drawn.
