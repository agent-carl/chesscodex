The Classical Variation of the [Pirc Defense](/openings/pirc-defense) is 1.e4 d6 2.d4 Nf6 3.Nc3 g6 4.Nf3, also known as the Classical System or the Two Knights System. The usual continuation is 4...Bg7 5.Be2 O-O 6.O-O.

### The idea

White is content with the classical centre of pawns on e4 and d4 and gives up the committal f2-f4 of the [Austrian Attack](/openings/pirc-defense-austrian-attack). Black castles and builds a compact structure.

### Who plays it

Efim Geller, Anatoly Karpov and Evgeni Vasiukov have all used the system successfully as White, while Zurab Azmaiparashvili has scored well against it as Black.

### Transposition to the Dragon

After 4...Bg7 5.Be2 O-O 6.O-O, the move 6...c5 7.Be3 cxd4 8.Nxd4 transposes to the [Dragon Variation](/openings/sicilian-defense-dragon-variation) of the Sicilian Defense.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 48.0% of the 4.6 million games and Black 46.8%. The gap is small under 1400, 49.1% against 46.4%, and wider from 2200 up, 50.0% against 42.8%. In 5,640 master games over the board White won 32.6%, Black 23.8%, and 43.6% were drawn.
