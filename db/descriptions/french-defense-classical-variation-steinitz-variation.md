The Steinitz Variation is 1.e4 e6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.e5, a line of the [Classical Variation](/openings/french-defense-classical-variation) of the [French Defense](/openings/french-defense) named after Wilhelm Steinitz.

### The idea

White closes the centre with 4.e5, and the knight retreats with 4...Nfd7. In the main lines Black's play is directed against White's d4-pawn: ...c5 and ...Nc6, and often ...Qb6, step up the pressure on it.

### Main lines

After 4...Nfd7:

- 5.f4 c5 6.Nf3 Nc6 7.Be3 is the main line, the [Boleslavsky Variation](/openings/french-defense-steinitz-variation-boleslavsky-variation) on this site. Black can step up the pressure on d4 with 7...Qb6 or 7...cxd4 8.Nxd4 Qb6, start queenside play with 7...a6 8.Qd2 b5, or continue developing the kingside with 7...Be7 or 7...cxd4 8.Nxd4 Bc5. White then chooses between Qd2 with O-O-O and Be2 with O-O; the first usually gives sharp play with castling on opposite sides.
- 5.Nce2, the Shirov-Anand Variation, prepares to support the centre with c2-c3 and f2-f4.
- 5.Nf3 gives a position also reached from the [Two Knights Variation](/openings/french-defense-two-knights-variation), 2.Nf3 d5 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.e5 Nfd7 5.d4.

### A trap in the main line

After 5.f4 c5 6.Nf3 Nc6 the natural-looking 7.Be2? cxd4 8.Nxd4 Ndxe5! 9.fxe5 Qh4+ wins a pawn for Black.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 48.0% of the 4.1 million games and Black 47.2%. White's lead grows with rating, from 50.5% against 46.0% under 1400 to 49.5% against 43.9% from 2200 up. In 16,347 master games over the board White won 34.4%, Black 19.5%, and 46.0% were drawn.
