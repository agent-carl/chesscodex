The Stonewall Variation of the [Dutch Defense](/openings/dutch-defense) is the set-up in which Black plays ...d5 early and secures a tight grip on the e4-square. On this page it is reached by 1.d4 f5 2.c4 Nf6 3.g3 e6 4.Bg2 Be7 5.Nf3 O-O 6.O-O d5; another typical move order is 1.d4 f5 2.g3 Nf6 3.Bg2 e6 4.Nf3 d5 5.c4 c6.

### Origins

The opening's attacking potential is shown in the Polish Immortal, in which Miguel Najdorf, playing the Stonewall, sacrificed all of his minor pieces and won by checkmate.

The Stonewall enjoyed a resurgence of interest in the 1980s and 1990s, when Artur Yusupov, Sergey Dolmatov, Nigel Short and Simen Agdestein helped develop the system in which Black plays ...d5 earlier and puts the dark-squared bishop on d6. This Modern Stonewall has remained more popular than the traditional early ...Be7 seen on this page. Magnus Carlsen has used the Stonewall to win games against Viswanathan Anand and Fabiano Caruana.

### The idea

Against the Dutch, White most often fianchettoes the king's bishop with g3 and Bg2. After 1.d4 f5 2.g3 Nf6 3.Bg2 e6 4.Nf3 Be7 5.O-O O-O 6.c4, Black chooses between 6...d5, the characteristic move of the Stonewall, 6...d6, the Ilyin–Zhenevsky Variation, and Alekhine's 6...Ne4!?, which keeps the option of moving the d-pawn one or two squares. With pawns on f5, e6 and d5, Black controls e4.

### Main lines

- [7.Nc3 c6](/openings/dutch-defense-stonewall-variation-3) is classified as ECO A95.
- 7.b3 is the [Botvinnik Variation](/openings/dutch-defense-stonewall-variation-botvinnik-variation) (ECO A93), and [7.b3 c6 8.Ba3](/openings/dutch-defense-stonewall-variation-4) is classified as A94.
- In the Modern Stonewall Black plays ...d5 earlier and develops the dark-squared bishop to d6 instead of e7.

### Who should play it

The Stonewall suits Black players who want a fixed pawn structure against 1.d4 with a firm hold on the e4-square.

### The numbers

In 37,434 rated Lichess games between players rated 1600 and 2500 that reached this position, White won 50.5%, Black 41.9%, and 7.6% were drawn. After 7.Nc3 c6 the figures over 150,082 games are 50.1% for White, 42.7% for Black and 7.2% draws.
