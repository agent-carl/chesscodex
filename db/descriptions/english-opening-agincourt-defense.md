The Agincourt Defense (1.c4 e6) answers the [English Opening](/openings/english-opening) by preparing a supported ...d5, much as in the [French Defense](/openings/french-defense). It is a flexible move that often transposes to other openings.

### The idea

Black intends 2...d5 and a solid centre. White usually replies 2.Nc3 or 2.Nf3, and the game most often transposes to the [Queen's Gambit Declined](/openings/queens-gambit-declined) or the [Catalan Opening](/openings/catalan-opening), or to lines of the [Anglo-Indian Defense](/openings/english-opening-anglo-indian-defense) if Black plays ...Nf6. For a Black player who wants a Queen's Gambit Declined against 1.c4, 1...e6 and 2...d5 is the most accurate move order: the game transposes if White plays d4. After 1...Nf6 2.Nc3 e6, by contrast, White can avoid the QGD with 3.e4.

### Main lines

- 2.Nf3 d5 3.g3 Nf6 4.Bg2, the Neo-Catalan, similar to the Catalan Opening. Black either opens the game with 4...dxc4, the Neo-Catalan Accepted, usually followed by 5.Qa4+ and 6.Qxc4, or plays the quieter 4...Be7, the [Neo-Catalan Declined](/openings/english-opening-agincourt-defense-neo-catalan-declined).
- Catalan-type lines with ...Nf6 and ...d5, such as the [Semi-Slav set-up](/openings/english-opening-agincourt-defense-catalan-defense-semi-slav-defense) with ...c6 and the [Catalan Defense Accepted](/openings/english-opening-agincourt-defense-catalan-defense-accepted) with ...dxc4.
- 2.Nf3 d5 3.b3, heading for a [Réti Opening](/openings/reti-opening) instead of a Queen's Gambit.
- 2.d4, transposing to 1.d4 e6 2.c4 lines such as the Queen's Gambit Declined, the Dutch or the [English Defense](/openings/english-defense).

### Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths**

- A precise move order for reaching a Queen's Gambit Declined against 1.c4.
- Solid, French-like structures.

**Weaknesses**

- White is not obliged to play d4 and can choose Réti or Catalan set-ups.
- Black must be ready for several structures.

### Who should play it

The Agincourt suits Black players with a Queen's Gambit Declined or French repertoire who want to keep similar structures against 1.c4.

### The numbers

On Lichess White leads by about 5 points at every level: 50.6% against 45.6% under 1400, 50.6% against 45.2% at 1400–1799, 49.5% against 44.9% at 1800–2199 and 48.1% against 43.5% from 2200 up. In about 30,900 master games White won 36.0%, Black 21.7%, and 42.3% were drawn.
