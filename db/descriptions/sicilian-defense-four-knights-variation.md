The Four Knights Variation of the [Sicilian Defense](/openings/sicilian-defense) is 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 e6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 Nf6 5.Nc3 Nc6. It can also arise from the [Old Sicilian](/openings/sicilian-defense-old-sicilian): 2...Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 Nf6 5.Nc3 e6.

### The idea

The variation can serve as a route into the main line of the Sveshnikov, reached after 6.Ndb5 d6 7.Bf4 e5 8.Bg5 a6 9.Na3 b5. The point of this move order is to avoid White's options against the usual Sveshnikov move order, such as the [Rossolimo Variation](/openings/sicilian-defense-nyezhmetdinov-rossolimo-attack), 2...Nc6 3.Bb5, or 7.Nd5 in the standard Sveshnikov.

### Main lines

- 6.Ndb5 is the main move. Besides 6...d6, heading for the Sveshnikov, Black's main alternative is 6...Bb4. Then 7.a3 Bxc3+ 8.Nxc3 d5 9.exd5 exd5 gives a position where Black has given up the bishop pair but has active pieces and the chance of ...d5-d4.
- [6...Bc5](/openings/sicilian-defense-four-knights-variation-cobra-variation), the Cobra Variation, has the main line 7.Bf4 O-O 8.Bc7 Qe7 9.Bd6 Bxd6 10.Qxd6, often followed by the retreat 10...Qe8.
- [6.Nxc6](/openings/sicilian-defense-four-knights-variation-exchange-variation) bxc6 7.e5 Nd5 8.Ne4 is an option this move order gives White, so White is not obliged to enter the Sveshnikov. It has become more popular over time but is still less popular than 6.Ndb5.

### The numbers

On Lichess Black wins 50.0% of the 3.9 million games and White 44.5%. Black leads 51.8% to 44.8% under 1400; from 2200 up the results are close, 46.1% for Black against 45.7% for White. In 11,595 master games over the board White won 35.4%, Black 20.8%, and 43.8% were drawn.
