The Damiano Variation of the [Petrov's Defense](/openings/petrovs-defense) is 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nf6 3.Nxe5 Nxe4. Black copies White's capture instead of first driving the knight back with 3...d6. It is usually regarded as an error, though it has occasionally been seen in recent grandmaster play, and Black must play accurately to avoid an immediately losing position.

### Moves to avoid after 4.Qe2

After 4.Qe2, which attacks the knight on e4 along the e-file, there are several moves Black must avoid:

- 4...Nf6? 5.Nc6+ wins the queen with a discovered check.
- 4...d5 5.d3 Qe7 6.dxe4 Qxe5 7.exd5, and Black loses a pawn.
- 4...d6? 5.Nxf7 Kxf7 6.Qxe4, and Black has lost a pawn and the right to castle.

### The best defence

Best is 4...Qe7 5.Qxe4 d6 6.d4 dxe5 7.dxe5 Nc6. After 8.Nc3 Qxe5 9.Qxe5+ Nxe5 10.Nb5, or 10.Bf4, White has a moderate advantage. The position after 4...Qe7 has its own page here, the [Kholmov Gambit](/openings/petrovs-defense-damiano-variation-kholmov-gambit).

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 54.2% of the 1.1 million games and Black 40.6%. White's results fall as ratings rise: White wins 61.8% under 1400 and 62.0% between 1400 and 1799, but from 2200 up Black is ahead, 47.7% against 41.6%. In 375 master games over the board 56.0% were drawn, White won 31.7% and Black 12.3%.
