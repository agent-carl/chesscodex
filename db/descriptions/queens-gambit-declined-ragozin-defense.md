The Ragozin Defense is 1.d4 d5 2.c4 e6 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.Nf3 Bb4, a line of the [Queen's Gambit Declined](/openings/queens-gambit-declined) in which Black pins the c3-knight as in the [Nimzo-Indian](/openings/nimzo-indian-defense). This page reaches it by 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.Nf3 d5 4.Nc3 Bb4. It arises from the Three Knights Variation and is covered by ECO codes D37 to D39.

### Main lines

White's main options are 5.Bg5, 5.cxd5 and 5.Qa4+.

- 5.Bg5. Black can play 5...h6, 5...Nbd7 or [5...dxc4](/openings/queens-gambit-declined-ragozin-defense-vienna-variation), which leads to the Vienna Variation, commonly 6.e4 c5 7.Bxc4 cxd4 8.Nxd4. After 5...h6 the usual line is 6.Bxf6 Qxf6 7.e3 O-O 8.Rc1 dxc4 9.Bxc4 c5 10.O-O cxd4 11.Ne4 Qe7; 6.Bh4 is well met by 6...dxc4. After 5...Nbd7 play most often continues 6.e3 c5, the Westphalian Variation.
- 5.cxd5 exd5 6.Bg5 h6 7.Bh4 O-O 8.e3 Bf5. This avoids a possible transposition into the Vienna.
- [5.Qa4+](/openings/queens-gambit-declined-ragozin-defense-alekhine-variation), the Alekhine Variation. The usual continuation is 5...Nc6 6.e3 O-O, followed by 7.Qc2 or 7.Bd2.

### The idea

The bishop on b4 pins the knight on c3 and can be exchanged for it, as in the Nimzo-Indian, while Black keeps the classical pawn on d5. In several lines Black takes on c4 and then strikes at the centre with ...c5.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 53.7% of the 4.3 million games and Black 41.1%. Below 2200 White wins about 53 to 54%, and from 2200 up 49.6% against 42.5%. Masters over the board draw most games: in 18,247 games 59.0% were drawn, White won 23.7% and Black 17.3%.
