The Tartakower Variation, also known as the Korchnoi Variation, is 1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 dxe4 4.Nxe4 Nf6 5.Nxf6+ exf6, a line of the [Caro-Kann Defense](/openings/caro-kann-defense). After White exchanges knights on f6, Black recaptures with the e-pawn.

### The idea

5...exf6 offers Black rapid development. In return Black cedes White the better pawn structure and long-term prospects, and has to be careful that White's d-pawn can become a passed pawn in the endgame.

It is regarded as sounder than the other recapture, 5...gxf6, the [Bronstein-Larsen Variation](/openings/caro-kann-defense-bronstein-larsen-variation).

### Who plays it

Viktor Korchnoi played it many times, including in his first world championship match against Anatoly Karpov, and Ulf Andersson has also used it.

Other lines after 3...dxe4 4.Nxe4 are the [Classical Variation](/openings/caro-kann-defense-classical-variation), 4...Bf5, and the [Karpov Variation](/openings/caro-kann-defense-karpov-variation), 4...Nd7.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 47.3% of the 3.2 million games and Black 45.8%. Under 1400 Black is slightly ahead, 48.2% against 47.8%; from 2200 up White leads 47.1% to 44.1%. In 4,116 master games over the board White won 30.4%, Black 26.2%, and 43.4% were drawn.
