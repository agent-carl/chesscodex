The Boi Variation, also called the Classical Variation, is 1.e4 e5 2.Bc4 Bc5, Black's symmetrical reply to the [Bishop's Opening](/openings/bishops-opening).

### The idea

White can transpose to other openings. With 3.Nc3 White enters the [Vienna Game](/openings/vienna-game), and 3.Nf3 Nc6 is the [Giuoco Piano](/openings/italian-game-giuoco-piano). After 3.d3 the game can also turn into the King's Gambit Declined or the Giuoco Pianissimo. White can keep the game in the Bishop's Opening with the Philidor Variation, 3.c3.

### Main lines

- [3.c3](/openings/bishops-opening-philidor-variation), the Philidor Variation, has the main line 3...Nf6 4.d4 exd4 5.e5 d5 6.exf6 dxc4 7.Qh5 O-O 8.Qxc5 Re8+ 9.Ne2 d3 10.Be3. Black's most energetic answer is [3...d5](/openings/bishops-opening-lewis-countergambit), the Lewis Countergambit, named after the English player William Lewis, who published analysis of it in 1834.
- [3.b4](/openings/bishops-opening-mcdonnell-gambit), the Wing Gambit, gives positions like those of the [Evans Gambit](/openings/italian-game-evans-gambit) and often transposes into it, for instance by 3...Bxb4 4.c3 Ba5 5.Nf3 Nc6.
- 3.Qf3 and 3.Qh5 are popular among lower-rated players because they threaten Scholar's Mate on f7. The threat is easily met, for example 3.Qh5 Qe7, and the moves are considered inferior, since they hamper White's development or leave the queen exposed and lose time.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 51.2% of the 6.6 million games and Black 44.6%. White's lead is largest under 1400, 54.1% against 41.8%, and from 2200 up it is 50.7% against 43.6%. There are too few master games over the board, 175, to compare.
