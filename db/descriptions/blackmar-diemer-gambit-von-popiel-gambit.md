The von Popiel Gambit is 1.d4 d5 2.e4 dxe4 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.Bg5, an older form of the [Blackmar-Diemer Gambit](/openings/blackmar-diemer-gambit) in which White pins the knight instead of playing the usual 4.f3.

### History

Ignatz von Popiel played 4.Bg5 in 1889. He came up with the idea of 3.Nc3 in place of the original [Blackmar Gambit](/openings/blackmar-diemer-gambit-blackmar-gambit) 3.f3, although his main idea was to meet 3...Nf6 with 4.Bg5 rather than 4.f3. He also analysed the Lemberger Countergambit, 3.Nc3 e5, which was common at the time. The later main line with 4.f3 was popularised by Emil Josef Diemer.

### 4...Bf5

The most common reply is 4...Bf5, adding a defender to the e4-pawn. A common trap follows 5.Qe2 Qxd4? 6.Qb5+, forking Black's king, the bishop on f5 and the b-pawn.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 51.5% of the 665,407 games and Black 44.2%. Below 1800 the results are close, about 48% against 47%, while from 1800 up White leads: 52.8% against 42.8% between 1800 and 2199, and 50.9% against 43.3% from 2200 up. There are only 20 master games, too few to judge.
