The Accelerated Dragon (1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 g6) is a [Sicilian Defense](/openings/sicilian-defense) in which Black fianchettoes the king's bishop, as in the [Dragon Variation](/openings/sicilian-defense-dragon-variation), but leaves the d-pawn at home. That lets Black play ...d5 in one move when the chance comes.

### The idea

Skipping ...d6 is the point of the move order. If White tries a Yugoslav Attack set-up with 5.Nc3 Bg7 6.Be3 Nf6 7.f3 O-O 8.Qd2, Black equalises at once with 8...d5!. When White does play 5.Nc3, it is usually with 6.Be3, 7.Bc4 and 8.Bb3, guarding against tricks with ...Nxe4 and ...d5, followed by kingside castling.

### Main lines

- 5.c4, the [Maróczy Bind](/openings/sicilian-defense-accelerated-dragon-maroczy-bind), the critical test of Black's move order. White's pawns on c4 and e4 restrain ...d5 and ...b5 and try to cramp Black. Play is less tactical than in many Sicilians, with a lot of strategic manoeuvring. The main line is 5...Bg7 6.Be3 Nf6 7.Nc3, when 7...O-O or 7...Ng4 ([Breyer Variation](/openings/sicilian-defense-accelerated-dragon-maroczy-bind-breyer-variation)) are most often played.
- 5.Nc3, the [Modern Variation](/openings/sicilian-defense-accelerated-dragon-modern-variation), usually with 5...Bg7 6.Be3 Nf6 7.Bc4 ([Modern Bc4 Variation](/openings/sicilian-defense-accelerated-dragon-modern-bc4-variation)).
- 5.Nxc6, the [Exchange Variation](/openings/sicilian-defense-accelerated-dragon-exchange-variation).
- The same positions can be reached through the [Hyperaccelerated Dragon](/openings/sicilian-defense-hyperaccelerated-dragon), 2...g6.

### Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths**

- Black can answer the Yugoslav Attack set-up with an immediate ...d5.
- A Dragon-style fianchetto with the bishop on g7.

**Weaknesses**

- The Maróczy Bind (5.c4) restrains Black's ...d5 and ...b5 breaks.
- Positions after 5.c4 call for long strategic manoeuvring rather than tactics.

### Who should play it

The Accelerated Dragon suits Black players who like the Dragon's fianchetto but want to avoid the sharpest Yugoslav Attack lines, and who are comfortable defending against the Maróczy Bind.

### The numbers

On Lichess Black does better up to 2200: 50.6% against 46.0% under 1400, 49.0% against 47.0% at 1400–1799 and 47.9% against 46.6% at 1800–2199. From 2200 up the results are level, 45.9% for Black against 45.7%. In about 13,900 master games White won 34.4%, Black 23.0%, and 42.6% were drawn.
