The Smith-Morra Gambit Accepted is 1.e4 c5 2.d4 cxd4 3.c3 dxc3, Black's acceptance of the [Smith-Morra Gambit](/openings/sicilian-defense-smith-morra-gambit). After 4.Nxc3 White has a piece developed and a pawn in the centre, while Black has an extra pawn and a central pawn majority. The gambit is popular at club level and played occasionally by masters.

### White's plan

White's plan is straightforward: put the king's bishop on c4 against f7, control the c- and d-files with rooks, and use the rapid development and open lines while Black struggles to find a good square for the queen. A typical scheme is Nf3, Bc4, O-O, Qe2, Rfd1 and Rac1. White also tries to stop or delay Black's castling, which opens the door to sacrifices such as Nd5, especially strong with a black pawn on e6, as well as Nxb5 and Bxe6.

### Black's set-ups

After 4.Nxc3 Black has many possible formations:

- 4...Nc6 5.Nf3 d6 6.Bc4 e6 7.O-O Nf6 8.Qe2 Be7 9.Rd1 e5 is the classical main line, a Scheveningen formation.
- In the Siberian Variation, 4...Nc6 5.Nf3 e6 6.Bc4 Nf6 7.O-O Qc7 8.Qe2 Ng4, 9.h3? loses to the famous Siberian Trap, 9...Nd4, winning the queen.
- Other set-ups include the Paulsen, Kan and Taimanov formations with ...e6 and ...a6, the Fianchetto Formation with ...g6, and the Chicago Defense, in which Black plays ...a6, ...b5 and ...Ra7.

White can also play 4.Bc4 or 4.Nf3 instead of recapturing; after 4...cxb2 5.Bxb2 the play is similar to the Danish Gambit.

### Black's task

To survive against a well-prepared opponent Black must first get through the traps of the opening, then hold out against White's long-term pressure. If Black manages this while keeping the extra pawn, the endgame offers good winning chances, but it is not easy, and many Sicilian players decline the gambit altogether.

### The numbers

On Lichess White wins 51.3% of the 9.2 million games and Black 44.7%. From 2200 up the results are closer, 48.4% against 46.0%. Masters have done well with Black: in 1,528 games over the board Black won 36.0%, White 23.1%, and 40.9% were drawn.
