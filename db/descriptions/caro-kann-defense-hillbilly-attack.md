The Hillbilly Attack is 1.e4 c6 2.Bc4, an offbeat answer to the [Caro-Kann Defense](/openings/caro-kann-defense). It has an inferior reputation, because after 2...d5 3.exd5 cxd5 White must move the bishop again and loses a tempo. It has nevertheless emerged as a surprise weapon in online blitz.

### The gambit with 3.Bb3

The reason is the gambit line 2...d5 3.Bb3, advocated by Simon Williams. If Black accepts the pawn with 3...dxe4, White may play 4.Qh5, aiming at f7, which is defended only by Black's king. Black usually blunts the threat with 4...g6 or 4...e6.

### The name

Williams attributes the name to National Master Jack Young, who allegedly used it to describe the unrefined nature of the move and claimed to have invented it in 1988. Williams played it as early as 2011 and featured it in his "Spicy Gambits" series in 2015.

### The numbers

On Lichess Black wins 52.9% of the 6.3 million games and White 42.7%. Black's lead holds through most rating bands, but from 2200 up the results are nearly even, 47.1% for Black against 46.9%. There are only 19 master games, too few to judge.
