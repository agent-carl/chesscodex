1.e4, the King's Pawn Game, is the most popular first move in chess, ahead of [1.d4](/openings/queens-pawn-game). The pawn takes a central square, attacks d5 and opens lines for the king's bishop and the queen. Bobby Fischer called it "best by test".

### The idea

With 1.e4 White claims the centre straight away and prepares quick development: the bishop can come out to c4 or b5, the knight to f3, and White can castle early. The e4-pawn is not protected yet, so it becomes a target for Black's first replies. Every major defence is a different way of dealing with it.

### Black's main answers

- 1...e5, the Open Games: the [Italian Game](/openings/italian-game), [Ruy Lopez](/openings/ruy-lopez), [Scotch Game](/openings/scotch-game), [King's Gambit](/openings/kings-gambit) and more.
- 1...c5, the [Sicilian Defense](/openings/sicilian-defense): an unbalanced fight for d4.
- 1...e6, the [French Defense](/openings/french-defense): ...d5 next, with a solid pawn chain.
- 1...c6, the [Caro-Kann Defense](/openings/caro-kann-defense): ...d5 next, keeping the light-squared bishop free.
- 1...d6, the [Pirc Defense](/openings/pirc-defense), and 1...g6, the [Modern Defense](/openings/modern-defense): Black lets White build a centre and attacks it later.
- 1...d5, the [Scandinavian Defense](/openings/scandinavian-defense): an immediate challenge to e4.
- 1...Nf6, the [Alekhine Defense](/openings/alekhine-defense): the knight provokes the e-pawn forward.

### Rare replies

Among the rarer answers are the [Nimzowitsch Defense](/openings/nimzowitsch-defense) 1...Nc6, the [Owen Defense](/openings/owen-defense) 1...b6, and the [St. George Defense](/openings/st-george-defense) 1...a6, with which Tony Miles beat Anatoly Karpov in 1980.

### Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths**

- Fast development and early castling.
- Open, tactical positions are common, especially after 1...e5.
- Every major defence is well studied, so there is plenty of material to learn from.

**Weaknesses**

- The e4-pawn is a target from move one.
- Black can choose the character of the game: a sharp Sicilian, a closed French or a quiet Caro-Kann.

### Who should play it

1.e4 suits almost everyone, and it is the classic recommendation for beginners, because the open games teach development, the centre and tactics. Stronger players choose it when they are ready to meet each of Black's defences with a prepared line.

### The numbers

1.e4 is the most-played first move on Lichess. White wins 48.1–50.2% and Black 44.8–46.5% at every rating band; draws rise from 4% under 1400 to 7.1% from 2200 up. In master games over the board, White wins 32%, Black 24.2% and 43.8% are drawn.
